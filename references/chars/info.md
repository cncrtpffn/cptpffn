## Style notes (common to all characters)
Exact geometry and proportions (for modeling and prompts): [style-geometry.md](style-geometry.md).

- Built from 4 separate parts: head ball (face, headwear), body (clothes only, flattened teardrop shape), two hand spheres. No legs, no arms.
- Hands are free-floating spheres beside the body (no arm geometry).
- Head is a separate ball resting on the body; face = two small black dot eyes (sometimes a short line for a closed/winking eye), no mouth or nose most of the time.
- Thick black outline (toon / inverted-hull look), flat-ish cel shading with soft gradient.
- Skin tone: light tan/beige. Clothes are drawn on the body (collar, button placket, pocket seams).
